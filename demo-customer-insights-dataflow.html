📌 Node Details: Salesforce Health Score Engine
The Health Score is Apsona's primary account metric (0–100), calculated using three weighted inputs: Login Count per License (55%), Feature Coverage Ratio (35%), and Support Case Volume (10%). It feeds directly into the AI Risk Model to trigger preemptive outreach before renewal dates.
